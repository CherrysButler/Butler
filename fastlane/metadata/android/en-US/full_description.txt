A third party client for JanitorAI that's all about smoothness, quality-of-life stuff, and not falling over. You sign in with your own JanitorAI account.

<b>Smooth</b>
• Native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Tabs switch in a single frame and long chats scroll without stuttering.
• Replies stream in smoothly, and it's light on RAM and battery.

<b>Chatting</b>
• A chat that's nice to read: Story, Bubbles, or Janitor-style layouts, and a reading font with real italics.
• Readable thinking, with an animation while the model thinks and the full thought process in its own sheet.
• Guided swipes: tell the model what to change ("shorter", "more dialogue") before it rerolls.
• Choices: short next moves from your model or JLLM, one tap to send.
• Better error handling: your message is saved before it's sent, failed replies retry with a countdown, and a reply that dies halfway can be continued.
• Edit anything, including the first message.

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Themes: Janitor Classic, Lights out (true black) and Daylight.
• Pick your own colors for speech, thoughts and actions.
• Swap your default persona, with backups.
• Pin and archive chats, Telegram style, saved to your account so they follow you.

<b>Just yours</b>
• Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
• Replies finish in the background, even on phones that kill background apps.

<b>Proxy nerd stuff</b>
• Clone proxy presets, key and all.
• OpenRouter presets, provider order, fallbacks, and sorting by price, speed or latency.
• See how full your context is from inside the chat.

<b>And the rest</b>
Sign in with Google, Discord or e-mail; export and import chats (Butler's format or SillyTavern's); block characters, creators, tags and keywords; notifications and their settings; published chats with reactions and comments.

Butler only talks to JanitorAI (and your own proxy, if you set one). It has no server of its own, no analytics and no crash reporting; if something breaks, you can make a report in Settings and send it yourself.

Butler is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JanitorAI.
