Same account, less of your data.

Butler talks to Janitor, keeps what it needs on your phone, and sends nothing anywhere else. Here's the whole picture, and how to check it.

Where your traffic goes

No Butler server, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ads. The official app ships Sentry, Statsig and Firebase; Butler ships none of them.

  • janitorai.com and its subdomains

    The API, chat generation, notifications and pictures.

  • auth.janitorai.com

    Janitor's login service, where your account lives.

  • challenges.cloudflare.com

    Only while you sign in with e-mail, because Janitor requires Cloudflare's check for it.

  • Your own proxy, if you set one

    OpenRouter or wherever you point it. Your messages go there because you told them to.

  • api.github.com, only if you ask

    Checks whether a newer Butler is out. Off until you turn it on, and it asks nothing about you.

What stays on your phone

Signing out deletes all of it. Pins, archive and folders live on your Janitor account, so they come back when you sign in again.

key

Your session

The tokens that prove you're you, encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore. Never in plain storage.

chat

A mirror of your chats

So screens paint instantly and work offline. The chats themselves live on your Janitor account.

save

Messages not sent yet

Written to the phone before any network call. That's the whole point.

settings

Your settings

The look, the chat layout, fonts, which proxy is selected, OpenRouter options.

cookie

Cloudflare's cookies for janitorai.com

The pass you earn at sign-in, sent back to Janitor the way a browser would. Cleared when you sign out.

What it asks Android for

No contacts, location, storage, camera, microphone or accessibility. Pictures and font files come through the system picker, which hands over only the file you chose.

wifi

Internet and network state

To talk to Janitor and your proxy, and to know when the connection is back.

notifications

Notifications

A reply that lands while you're away tells you so. Say no and it still finishes, quietly.

bolt

Foreground service and wake lock

What lets a reply finish in the background. Held only while a reply is being written.

battery_saver

Battery optimisation, sometimes

Asked once, only on phones that kill background apps anyway. Butler works without it.

Check it yourself

Put your phone behind mitmproxy, or any proxy that shows hostnames, and use Butler for a while. The list above is the whole list. If you ever see another host, that's a bug, and it should be reported.

When something goes wrong, Butler doesn't phone home. Settings › App › Report a problem builds a page of text on your phone, with anything shaped like a token, key or e-mail blanked out, and you decide whether to share it.
