Android 8 and up · Install steps and every release

  1. Download the APK

    The red key gets the newest one, straight from GitHub.

  2. Open it

    Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it once; that's normal for anything outside the Play Store.

  3. Install

    It installs over any earlier Butler and keeps your chats and settings.

  4. Sign in

    With your Janitor account: Google, Discord or e-mail. Cloudflare may ask you to tap its box once.

Check it yourself

You don't have to take the panel's word for it. On a computer, these two commands show the same numbers.

tag

The checksum

Should match the SHA-256 on the release page and in its SHA256SUMS.txt.

sha256sum Butler-0.4.1.apk
verified

The signature

The certificate's SHA-256 digest should read 664e07a5…f61cb5c1562d, the same for every release.

apksigner verify --print-certs Butler-0.4.1.apk
