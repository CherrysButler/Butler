Pictures in your message BETA

Put a picture in your line and a model of yours that can see writes it in, in your voice, right where you put it. You read it, then send. Nothing of the picture goes to Janitor.

Agent mode BETA

The reply is drafted, checked against goals strictly, fixed where it fails, and only then delivered. You pick the effort and add your own goals. For reasoning models on a proxy.

It never loses your words.

Your message is saved before it's sent. When the connection drops, Butler says so and tries again by itself, with a countdown, instead of a red flash and a Retry you have to find.

  • QueuedSaved on the phone, waiting for the network.
  • RetryingA visible countdown, and Retry now if you're impatient.
  • Cut shortYou keep what came in, and Continue picks it up.

A demo, not a real chat.

It talks to Janitor, and nothing else.

There is no Butler server, no analytics and no crash reporter. This is the whole list of places your phone talks to.

The long versionarrow_forward
  • janitorai.com and its subdomains

    The API, chat generation, notifications and pictures.

  • auth.janitorai.com

    Janitor's login service, where your account lives.

  • Your own proxy, if you set one

    OpenRouter or wherever you point it. Your messages go there because you told them to.

  • api.github.com, only if you ask

    Checks whether a newer Butler is out. Off until you turn it on, and it asks nothing about you.
