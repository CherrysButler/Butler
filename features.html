Butler specials

Butter, Highlights, Pictures and Agent mode run on your own model, so they cost tokens, and how well they work depends on how well your model follows instructions.

auto_awesome

Description writer

Write a few lines about your persona, pick one of your proxy presets, and it fleshes them out in your own style, keeping everything you wrote.

image

Pictures in your message BETA

Put a picture in your line and a model of yours that can see writes it in, in your voice, right where you put it. You read it, then send. Nothing of the picture goes to Janitor.

loop

Agent mode BETA

The reply is drafted, checked against goals strictly, fixed where it fails, and only then delivered. You pick the effort and add your own goals. For reasoning models on a proxy.

chrome_reader_mode

A chat that's nice to read

Three layouts, Story, Bubbles or Janitor-style with avatars, and a reading font with real italics so *actions* look like actions.

psychology

Readable thinking

A proper animation while the model thinks, and the whole thought opens in its own sheet. The thinking words are yours to change: edit Claude's list or add your own.

refresh

Guided rerolls

Don't like a reply? Say what to change before it rerolls: shorter, more dialogue, slow down. They stack, and you can type your own.

checklist

Choices

Too lazy to type? Your model or JLLM offers a few short replies to pick from.

cloud_sync

Errors handled properly

Saved before it's sent, retried by itself with a countdown, and a reply cut short keeps what came in so Continue can finish it.

edit

Edit anything

Every message, including the first one, which the official app won't let you touch.

share

Share a bot

A share key on any character page hands its link to your phone's share sheet.

manage_accounts

Edit your persona mid-chat

A pencil beside each persona in Play as opens its editor, then you're back in the chat.

keyboard_hide

The keyboard goes down when you send

Unless you'd rather it stayed up.

One font for chats, one for the rest of the app. A handful built in, or add your own .ttf or .otf.

wallpaper

Backgrounds

view_list

Home your way

Endless scroll or pages, and it keeps your place when you come back from a character.

Just yours

fingerprint

Lock the app

With your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The recents screen shows a blank card and notifications hide what was said.

autorenew

Replies finish in the background

Lock your phone mid-reply and it's waiting when you come back, even on phones that love killing apps.
